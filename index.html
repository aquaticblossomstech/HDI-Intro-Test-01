How to send your sketch

This form prepares a message. It does not send it. You choose whether to share it by email or DM.

  1. Tap Copy sketch, or Download .txt. In-app browsers can block both, so use the text box above if needed.
  2. Send it in a direct message to @SupportNJLocal.
  3. Or tap Email it, or write to rahwayallday@gmail.com.

Keep sensitive material out

This page does not transmit your entries to a server. Your browser holds the form values while the page is open. Do not include passwords, private records, confidential client information, or sensitive personal details in a sketch you plan to share.

Discovery can stay open. The agreement should not.

If the work is a fit, purpose, scope, output, fee, and timing are agreed before substantive work begins.

LIMITS

What this isn’t

So expectations are clear from the start.
